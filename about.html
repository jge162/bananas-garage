About

The truck, and the guy who wrenches on it.

Banana is a 1979 GMC Sierra Grande. Pale yellow and cream, a soft two-tone that reads as banana in the right light, which is how the name landed.

She is a driver. Commute photos, a broken accelerator cable, oil on the driveway, the hood up for valvetrain work. The hood-open shot, with the Edelbrock air cleaner and chrome valve covers, is the same truck as the 5 a.m. one.

The owner

I work as an electrical engineer. I'm a family man. I'm a squarebody enthusiast who lives for adventure. That's the bio, and it still fits. Some adventure is a road. A lot of it is keeping a truck from 1979 useful.

Banana's Garage is the notebook. Instagram, @79_sierragrande, stays the place for the photos and the short clips. If you want to write, the email is bananasgarage79@gmail.com.
