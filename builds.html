Engine bay

Engine & valvetrain

The engine bay is red, and the work in there has been the valvetrain. A torque wrench on the covers, set it, then check it again. It's driveway time on a 1979 truck, hood up, day going by.

Under the truck

Oil pan & timing cover

The oil pan and timing cover came off and went back together with parts from a CUCV donor. The words on the post are the whole spec here: a quick drain before the install, CUCV 1031 donor. One squarebody keeping this one on the road.

Edelbrock air cleaner

With the bay buttoned up, the top of the engine wears an Edelbrock air cleaner and chrome valve covers. That's the finished view after the wrenching, the one you get when the hood is open at a stop. Chrome sitting over the work that happened in that red bay.
