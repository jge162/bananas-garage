Builds & DIY

Jobs on the truck.

Each card is a job that has been on Banana. The write-ups stay with what showed up in the driveway and on the grid: the red bay, the CUCV donor, the Edelbrock air cleaner, the JVC, the shoelaces, the early commute.

Engine Oil pan Top end Stereo Roadside Daily driving
01 Engine bay

Engine & valvetrain

The engine bay is red, and the work in there has been the valvetrain. A torque wrench on the covers, set it, then check it again. It's driveway time on a 1979 truck, hood up, day going by.

See it on Instagram, Engine and valvetrain (opens in a new tab)
02 Under the truck

Oil pan & timing cover

The oil pan and timing cover came off and went back together with parts from a CUCV donor. The words on the post are the whole spec here: a quick drain before the install, CUCV 1031 donor. One squarebody keeping this one on the road.

See it on Instagram, oil pan and timing cover (opens in a new tab)
03 Top end

Edelbrock air cleaner

With the bay buttoned up, the top of the engine wears an Edelbrock air cleaner and chrome valve covers. That's the finished view after the wrenching, the one you get when the hood is open at a stop. Chrome sitting over the work that happened in that red bay.

See it on Instagram, top end (opens in a new tab)
04 Cab

Stereo upgrade

An aftermarket JVC head unit sits in the dash. The cab is still a 1979 squarebody, and the drive to work has a soundtrack now. A small job, and one that gets used every morning.

See it on Instagram, stereo upgrade (opens in a new tab)
05 Roadside

Accelerator cable

The accelerator cable broke on the road. Shoelaces got me home. That's the whole story, and it stays in the log: Banana gets driven, and some repairs start with whatever is already in the cab.

See it on Instagram, roadside fixes (opens in a new tab)
06 On the road

Daily driving & night cruises

A lot of the miles are ordinary and early. The grid has a 5 a.m. roll call on the way to work, night drives with the cab dark, and sunset light on the Sierra Grande fender badge. The maintenance is so those drives keep happening.
